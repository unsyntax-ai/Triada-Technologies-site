Arrangements

Four ways to staff your team

Each arrangement balances speed, cost, and commitment differently. We will help you choose the one that fits your situation.

Short-term and project-based professionals who can start quickly and work to your schedule. Ideal for seasonal demand, special projects, and backfill coverage.

Process

How a placement works

Six clear steps, from the first conversation to a placement that keeps performing after the start date.

  1. Consultation

    We confirm the requirements, the technical skills, the team culture, the working schedule, and the timeline. A clear brief makes every later step faster.

  2. Sourcing

    We search our local candidate database, industry networks, and referrals to find people who match the role, including those who are not actively looking.

  3. Screening

    Each candidate completes skill reviews and behavioral interviews, and we verify background and credentials where the role requires it.

  4. Presentation

    You receive a short list with each candidate's qualifications, experience, and fit for your team, summarized so you can decide quickly.

  5. Placement

    We coordinate interviews, help with offer terms, and manage the paperwork and onboarding so the new hire starts on the right foot.

  6. Follow-up

    We check in with you and the new hire during the first weeks, so any concern is addressed early and the placement succeeds.
