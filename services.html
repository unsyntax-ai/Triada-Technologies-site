Process

How a placement works

  1. Consultation

    We confirm the requirements, the technical needs, the team culture, and the timeline.

  2. Sourcing

    We search our local database, industry networks, and passive candidates for matches.

  3. Screening

    Skill reviews, background checks, and behavioral interviews to filter applicants.

  4. Presentation

    A short list of candidates, with their qualifications and fit summarized for you.

  5. Placement

    We coordinate interviews, help with offer terms, and manage onboarding.

  6. Follow-up

    Regular check-ins with you and the hire to confirm the placement is working.
